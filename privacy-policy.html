Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

At Coastline Offers LLC, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you visit our website or submit your information.

What Information Do We Collect?

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you fill out a form, request information, or contact us.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property or service-related details
  • Any other information you choose to provide

We may also collect limited technical data automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage data.

How Do We Use Your Information?

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and contact you about your request
  • Provide services and evaluate your submission
  • Send marketing and informational communications (including SMS, where you have opted in)
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience

SMS & Mobile Information

If you provide your phone number and opt in, you agree to receive marketing and informational text messages from Coastline Offers LLC. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out at any time by texting STOP. For assistance, text HELP or contact us using the information below.

Mobile Data & Sharing Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your mobile information or SMS opt-in consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Do We Share Your Information?

We do not sell or trade your personal information.

We may share your information only:

  • With trusted service providers who help us operate our website or communicate with you (e.g., messaging platforms)
  • When required by law or to protect our rights

How Do We Protect Your Information?

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Online Use Only

This Privacy Policy applies only to information collected through our website and not to offline interactions.
